# Cash payout process
Cash payouts move from draft to prepared, awaiting confirmation, and confirmed received. Record a unique receipt reference, recipient identity, preparer, confirmer, date, amount, and linked commission IDs. Do not use cash as an undocumented substitute for a bank payout.

Configurable policy: whether cash is permitted, maximum amount, two-person approval, identification, and reconciliation cadence. Te controleren met jurist/boekhouder: cash-register, AML, tax, and recordkeeping obligations.
