# Payout flow
Only available commissions for one seller can form a payout. The system prevents a commission from appearing twice in a batch and records payout items at the commission amount. Finance approval and payment registration must be auditable and segregated from seller access.

Configurable policy: batch review thresholds, payout schedule, bank-file procedure, and approval limits. Te controleren met jurist/boekhouder: payout statements, withholding, and accounting treatment.
