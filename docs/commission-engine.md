# Commission engine
Amounts are integer euro cents. The engine supports fixed, percentage-of-sale, percentage-of-margin, and combined rules. It derives margin from sale less VAT, product cost, payment fee, and order costs; minimum, maximum, and maximum-margin-share limits can be applied.

A commission above available margin is marked for manual review. The database separately enforces verified payment, delivery, and configured hold time before availability. Configurable policy: rule versions, caps, and hold days. Te controleren met jurist/boekhouder: commission contract and VAT treatment.
