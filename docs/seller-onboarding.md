# Seller onboarding
Collect identity/contact details, business information where applicable, payout destination, agreement acceptance, and verification status. Validate that the seller is at least 18 and that the required agreement was accepted; do not approve based only on client-side validation.

Approval and administrative review criteria are configurable policy. Te controleren met
jurist/boekhouder: agreement wording, seller classification, and evidence retention.
External automatic IDV/KYC is **de-scoped for Partners v1** (no camera/document/selfie provider).
