# Privacy data map
Profiles hold identity and locale. Seller profiles and business records hold contact, payout, registration, and verification data. Customers hold contact and billing details. Orders, payments, commissions, payouts, support records, and audit events connect these records for operations and legal accounting.

Access is role-scoped and sellers cannot access other sellers' data, costs, ledgers, or customer records outside attributed orders. Te controleren met jurist/boekhouder: GDPR legal basis, controller/processor roles, notices, and data-subject handling.
