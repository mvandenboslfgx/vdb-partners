# Data retention policy
This is a template, not a final retention schedule. Retain financial, ledger, invoice, payout, and audit records for the legally required period; retain verification evidence only as long as necessary; delete or anonymize operational data when the retention purpose expires.

Configurable policy: exact periods, deletion jobs, legal holds, and restoration controls. Te controleren met jurist/boekhouder: Dutch/EU tax, accounting, GDPR, and AML retention requirements.
